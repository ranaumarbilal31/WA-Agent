How it works

  1. Export any WhatsApp chat (without media) and upload it — the analyzer learns your messages only, even in group chats.
  2. Pick a preset (Professional, Business, Friendly, Slang, Short) or use your Custom style per chat.
  3. Set mode per chat: Auto replies, Draft prepares, Off stays silent.
